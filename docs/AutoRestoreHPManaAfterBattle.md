# AutoRestoreHPManaAfterBattle

## Overview
**AutoRestoreHPManaAfterBattle** is a quality-of-life mod that automatically recovers your party's HP and MP to 100% upon concluding any combat encounter.

---

## Features
* **Zero Post-Battle Tedium**: Removes the repetitive chore of opening menus to spam Potions or low-tier curing spells after trash mob fights.
* **Full Combat Readiness**: Ensures every battle starts with your team in peak tactical condition.
* **Safe Trigger Timing**: Triggers only when the battle victory sequence has officially concluded, avoiding interference with mid-combat health checks.

---

## File Structure
* `Scripts/main.lua`: Battle end event listener and party stat restoration handler.
